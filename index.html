How the hybrid works

  1. Instant scan, no AI. Plain JavaScript totals the lines, spots duplicates and known junk-fee words. Works offline.
  2. Privacy shield. Card, account, phone and email numbers are masked on your device before any prompt is built.
  3. Firebase AI Logic, PREFER_ON_DEVICE. Chrome's built-in Gemini Nano answers when it's on this device. Otherwise the same request falls back to Gemini through the Gemini Developer API.
  4. Every answer is labelled from the SDK's inferenceSource: On this device or Cloud fallback.
  5. Math check. The AI's numbers are compared with the bill's own total, so a wrong answer can't slip by.

HushLedger is a hackathon project for #BuildHybridAI 2026. It is not legal or financial advice. Sample bills are fictional.
